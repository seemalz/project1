# 🫡 Master Plan for Seems — Age 36, Female

## 💪 Goal 1: Fitness (Post-Injury Recovery)

### Phase 1: Recovery Foundation (Weeks 1-2, starting March)
- **Walking:** 20-30 min daily at comfortable pace
- **Gentle stretching:** 10 min daily (focus on injured area + full body)
- **Core activation:** Basic planks, bird dog (2-3x/week)
- **Sleep:** 7-8 hours (critical for recovery)

### Phase 2: Strength Building (Weeks 3-8)
- **Strength training:** 3x/week, full body compound movements
  - Squats, deadlifts, push-ups, rows (start bodyweight → add resistance)
  - 2-3 sets, 12-15 reps initially
- **Cardio:** 150 min/week moderate intensity (Mayo Clinic guidelines)
  - Mix: walking, cycling, swimming
- **Active recovery:** Yoga/stretching 2x/week

### Phase 3: Performance & Maintenance (Week 9+)
- **Progressive overload:** Increase weight/reps weekly
- **Periodization:** 4-week cycles (build → deload)
- **Target:** 3-4 strength sessions + 2-3 cardio sessions/week

## 🥗 Goal 2: Nutrition (Evidence-Based)

### Foundation Principles
- **Protein:** 0.8-1.2g per kg body weight (muscle recovery + satiety)
- **Fiber:** 25g/day minimum (gut health, satiety)
- **Hydration:** Half body weight in ounces daily
- **80/20 rule:** 80% whole foods, 20% flexibility

### Weekly System
- **Meal prep:** 1-2 sessions/week (Sunday + Wednesday)
- **Grocery template:** Protein + vegetables + whole grains + healthy fats
- **Eating windows:** 3 meals + 1-2 snacks, consistent timing
- **Track:** First 4 weeks to establish patterns

### Sample Daily Framework
- **Breakfast:** Protein + fiber (eggs + vegetables, or Greek yogurt + berries)
- **Lunch:** Balanced macro plate (palm protein, 2 cups vegetables, complex carbs)
- **Dinner:** Lighter but complete (lean protein, vegetables, minimal processed carbs)
- **Snacks:** Protein + healthy fat (apple + almond butter, Greek yogurt)

## 🚀 Goal 3: Career (Strategic Advancement)

### Assessment Phase (Month 1)
- **Define promotion criteria:** Meet with manager, get specific requirements
- **Skill gap analysis:** What do you need that you don't have?
- **Internal networking:** Map key stakeholders and decision makers
- **Market research:** What are peers at target level doing/earning?

### Execution Phase (Months 2-6)
- **Stretch projects:** Volunteer for high-visibility work
- **Skill building:** 1 hour/week on promotion-critical skills
- **External visibility:** Industry events, LinkedIn content, professional associations
- **Mentorship:** Find sponsor at target level or above
- **Documentation:** Keep record of wins, metrics, positive feedback

### Promotion Campaign (Months 7-12)
- **Formal discussion:** Schedule promotion conversation with manager
- **Business case:** Quantified value you bring + future potential
- **Backup plan:** External opportunities if internal promotion stalls
- **Salary research:** Know your worth, negotiate confidently

## 🤝 Goal 4: Social (Systematic Reconnection)

### Audit Phase
- **Contact list:** All Canada friends you want to reconnect with
- **Priority tiers:** Close friends (immediate), good friends (month 2), acquaintances (month 3+)
- **Communication preferences:** Text, call, video, in-person visits

### Reconnection System
- **Week 1-2:** Text 2-3 closest friends
- **Week 3-4:** Schedule calls with those who respond positively
- **Monthly cadence:** Regular check-ins with core group (5-10 people)
- **Annual plan:** Visit Canada 1-2x/year for deeper connections

### Maintenance
- **Birthday calendar:** Never miss important dates
- **Group chat:** Consider creating one for core friend group
- **Shared activities:** Online games, watch parties, virtual events
- **Life updates:** Share your wins, ask about theirs

## 📋 Goal 5: Organization (Systems-Based)

### Inbox Zero System (Week 1)
- **Sprint:** 2-hour session to get to zero
- **Daily maintenance:** 15 minutes/day
- **4-action rule:** Reply, Task, Archive, Delete
- **Filters:** Automate routine emails

### Calendar Mastery (Week 2)
- **Audit:** Remove zombie recurring events
- **Time blocking:** 
  - Morning planning: 9:00-9:30am
  - Deep work: 2-hour blocks
  - Lunch break: actual break
  - End-of-day review: 5:00-5:30pm
- **Color coding:** Work, personal, health, social
- **Buffer time:** 15 min between meetings

### Task Management (Week 3)
- **Capture system:** One place for all tasks (with me initially)
- **Daily top 3:** Most important tasks each morning
- **Weekly review:** Sunday evening planning
- **Project tracking:** Break big goals into small tasks

### Weekly Rhythm (Week 4+)
- **Sunday:** Week preview, meal planning, goal review
- **Daily:** Morning priorities + evening reflection (via me)
- **Friday:** Week wrap-up, celebrate wins
- **Monthly:** Systems review and optimization
