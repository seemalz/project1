💪 Fitness: Post-Injury Recovery Protocol

Phase 1: Recovery Foundation (Weeks 1-2, starting March)

  • Walking 20-30 min daily at comfortable pace
  • Gentle stretching 10 min daily (focus on injured area + full body)
  • Core activation: Basic planks, bird dog (2-3x/week)
  • Sleep 7-8 hours consistently

Phase 2: Strength Building (Weeks 3-8)

  • Strength training 3x/week, full body compound movements
  • Cardio 150 min/week moderate intensity (Mayo Clinic guidelines)
  • Active recovery: Yoga/stretching 2x/week
  • Progressive overload system established

Phase 3: Performance & Maintenance (Week 9+)

  • 4-week periodization cycles (build → deload)
  • 3-4 strength + 2-3 cardio sessions/week
  • Target strength/physique achieved
